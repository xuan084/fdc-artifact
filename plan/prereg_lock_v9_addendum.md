## 0. Disclosures (deviations)

1. **The time-uniform guarantee is post hoc.** TU-FDC is the FDC-BF code (frozen since v7, no parameter changed) read under Theorem TU-1 (`plan/v9_candidates_theory.md`, external reviewer-reviewed in 2 rounds). The theorem was found after the v8 eval results. It strengthens the stated guarantee of an unchanged procedure. It is nevertheless a change of the confirmatory design: the former secondary dense-grid experiment becomes the v9 primary design, and TU-FDC is evaluated at 77 monitoring times through a new wrapper, which can change stopping times and sticky answers relative to K = 20 FDC-BF. The inherited width parameters are unchanged, and TU-FDC is numerically identical to FDC-BF when evaluated only on the K = 20 grid.
2. **Remaining guarantee asymmetry.** HC-WoR is time-uniform under any predictable sampling rule. TU-FDC and RECT-ck-BF-TU are time-uniform only under the frozen, outcome-free schedule. Methods use outcome-free, method-specific allocation schedules with shared arrivals and pool orders: X5 HC-WoR* uses its frozen Neyman plan and PJC uses deterministic count tracking. R2 checks the registered 50/50 group; R2c checks each method's own schedule digest and realised block counts across the dense and K = 20 grids; R2b checks arrivals.
3. **New rival.** RECT-ck-BF-TU is constructed for v9: the v8 matched Bennett rectangle (RECT-ck-BF+box) upgraded by the same Lemma TU, with radii frozen at the latest block point. It has no tuning parameter. It is identical to RECT-ck-BF+box on the block grid (unit test).
4. **Thresholds sized from dev.** 0.85 (A) and 0.50 (B) were set by the authors from dev dense-grid numbers, seeds 950–999: TU-FDC/HC-WoR* = 0.708 (UB 0.737) on CR9 and 0.356 (UB 0.365) on X5 (`plan/v9_candidates_report.md` §3.2). The v9 dev runner check below measured TU-FDC vs RECT-ck-BF-TU and vs PJC-local@D for the first time; it did not change any threshold.
5. **Table reuse.** The CR9 eval half is used for the 4th time with fresh streams (v5 30000–30199, v6 A 31000–31199, v7 A 33000–33199; v6 C and v8 C re-used existing streams). The X5 eval half is used for the 2nd time (v8 A, 35000–35199). Only the seeds are new.
6. **NO-GO candidates enter only as descriptive rows.** FDC-HG (exact-HG envelope; dev 0.97 / 0.98 vs FDC-BF) and FDC-LOC (gap-localised union; dev 0.925 / 1.000) failed their pre-stated go criteria (`plan/fdc_hg_dev_report.md`, `plan/v9_candidates_report.md`).
7. **Block C is descriptive only.** Hillstrom is an outcome-exposed log (`plan/hillstrom_exposure_audit.md`). The design B cost family (womens e-mail at half price) has no source and was the only cost family that met design rule B on the dev half. Design A is reported as a supplement.

8. **PJC asymmetry (external reviewer v9 r1 P1).** PJC-local@D carries its own K = 77 checkpoint ledger (an extra ln(77/20) ≈ 1.348 in β and smaller variance-box tail levels) but uses fresh widths, so its net cost is not predetermined. The no-reset, count-only PJC-local* also admits the Lemma-TU extension, so its time-uniform form **TU-PJC** (widths frozen at the K = 20 block points) is registered as a second non-inferiority rival in B. Non-inferiority wording is limited to these two registered PJC formulations; the matched K = 20 comparison is reported as before.
9. **Scope of confirmation (external reviewer v9 r1 P0).** Fresh streams of a reused finite table give inference conditional on that table, not independent replication across populations, and the rectangle comparison covers the two registered constructions only.

## 5. Replica and integrity

- **R1:** an independent process re-runs the first 10 seeds of each eval task; canonical rows (all fields except timing) identical.
- **R2:** schedule_digest identical across each registered frozen-design group per (seed, ε); in blocks A / B also across the frozen-design members of the d / k / x tasks.
- **R2b:** arrival_digest identical across all methods of a task, and across the tasks of a block (dense and K = 20 grids share the streams).
- **R2c (cross-grid):** for each method on both grids (TU-FDC/FDC-BF, HC-WoR, PJC-local, TU-PJC/PJC-local, RECT-ck-BF-TU/RECT-ck-BF+box, RECT-ck-HG, FDC-BF[K77]/FDC-BF, TU-LOC/FDC-LOC), the method's own schedule digest and its realised cell counts at every common block point are identical. This covers HC-WoR's Neyman schedule and PJC's count paths, which R2 does not. It enters the replica status of blocks A / B.
- Seals, single-commit lock anchor and gate cascade as in v8 (`v9_seal`, `prereg_v9.lock_history_check`).
